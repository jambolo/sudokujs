How to Use the App

This guide explains every button on the screen. Wherever it says tap, you can also click with a mouse.

The screen

From top to bottom, you will see:

  • The puzzle board. Squares with a grey background are part of the puzzle and cannot be changed. White squares are yours to fill in.
  • A row with three things in it: the ? button that opens this guide, a word in the middle that tells you what the app is currently doing (for example Highlighting or Pen), and the ✔ button that checks your work.
  • Two number pads. The black pad on the left is the pen, for entering your answers. The blue pad on the right is the pencil, for small notes.
  • The bottom row: a C (clear) button under each pad, the Menu button between them, and the undo ◄ and redo ► arrows in the corners. The arrows only appear when there is something to undo or redo.

Entering numbers

  1. Tap a number on the black pad on the left. It turns yellow, and the word in the middle changes to Pen.
  2. Tap any white square on the board to put that number in it.
  3. Keep tapping squares to place the same number again, or tap a different number on the pad.
  • To change a number, pick the new number and tap the square. The new number replaces the old one.
  • To erase a number, tap the C under the black pad, then tap the square.
  • To put the pen away, tap the selected number again.
  • Red numbers mean the same number appears twice in a row, column, or box. Both copies turn red so you can see the clash.
  • Greyed-out numbers on the pads have been placed nine times already, so you have used them all.

When every square is filled in with no clashes, the board lines turn green and a wave of color sweeps across the board. You solved it!

Making notes

Notes are small blue numbers that you can write in an empty square to remember which numbers might go there.

  1. Tap a number on the blue pad on the right. The word in the middle changes to Pencil.
  2. Tap an empty square to add that number as a note. Tap the square again to remove the note.
  • A square can hold as many notes as you like.
  • To erase all the notes in a square, tap the C under the blue pad, then tap the square.
  • To put the pencil away, tap the selected number again.
  • Notes are hidden while a square has a number in it. They come back if you erase the number.
  • The app never adds or removes notes for you. They are entirely yours to manage.

Highlighting

Highlighting helps you see what is going on without changing anything.

  • Highlight a square's row, column, and box. When no number is selected (the word in the middle says Highlighting), tap any square. That square turns yellow, and every square in the same row, column, and box turns pale yellow. Tap the square again to turn the highlight off.
  • Find every copy of a number. Double-tap (tap twice quickly) a number on the black pad. Every square containing that number lights up.
  • Find every note of a number. Double-tap a number on the blue pad. Every empty square with that note lights up.

Tapping an empty part of the screen, away from any button, puts away the pen or pencil and returns to Highlighting.

Checking your work

Tap the ✔ to the right of the board's bottom edge. The word in the middle changes to Checking, and the app marks mistakes in red:

  • A red number is wrong. It does not match the solution.
  • A red note can be crossed off, because that number is already correctly placed in the same row, column, or box.

Nothing is changed for you. The app only shows you where the problems are. To get back to solving, tap any number on either pad.

Undo and redo

  • Tap ◄ in the bottom left corner to take back your last change. Tap it again to go back further, all the way to the start of the puzzle if you like.
  • Tap ► in the bottom right corner to put back a change you just undid.
  • Both numbers and notes can be undone. The square that changed blinks so you can see what happened.
  • Undo and redo do nothing while the word in the middle says Links or Checking. Tap the word in the middle first.

Links

Links is an optional aid for harder puzzles. It draws lines between your notes, so it is only useful after you have filled in notes.

  1. Make sure no number is selected (the word in the middle says Highlighting), then tap that word. It changes to Links.
  2. Tap a number on the blue pad. Lines appear connecting the notes for that number that share a row, column, or box.
  • A thick line joins two notes that are the only two for that number in their row, column, or box. The number must go in one of those two squares.
  • A thin line joins notes where there are three or more possible squares.
  • Tap another number on the blue pad to see its links, or the C under the blue pad to hide the lines.
  • To leave Links, tap the word in the middle again.

Tapping the word in the middle always steps to the next mode, in this order: Highlighting, Links, Pencil, Pen, and back to Highlighting. Most of the time you will not need it, because tapping a number pad switches for you.

The menu

Tap Menu at the bottom of the screen to open it.

New GameStarts a new puzzle. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Extreme. Harder puzzles start with fewer numbers filled in.
Reset PuzzleErases all your numbers and notes so you can start the same puzzle over.
Load PuzzleOpens a puzzle that someone shared with you. Paste the puzzle code they sent, which starts with the letters SD. If the code is not accepted, check that you copied all of it, or press Cancel to give up.
Share PuzzleLets you send the puzzle you are playing to a friend as a puzzle code. Only the puzzle itself is shared, not the numbers and notes you have added.
ResumeCloses the menu and returns to your puzzle.

Careful: New Game, Reset Puzzle, and Load Puzzle take effect right away, without asking first, and your progress on the current puzzle cannot be brought back. If you opened the menu by accident, tap Resume.

The small figure in the bottom left corner of the menu, such as 40/81, shows how many of the puzzle's 81 squares were left for you to fill in. The bottom right corner shows the app's version.

Saving and installing

  • Your puzzle is saved automatically after every change. Close the app whenever you like. Next time you open it, everything is where you left it, including your notes and undo history.
  • The puzzle is saved on this device only, in the browser you are using. It will not appear on your other devices, and clearing your browser's data erases it. In a private or incognito window it may not be saved at all.
  • You can install the app on a phone or tablet. Open your browser's menu and choose Add to Home Screen or Install. It then opens like any other app and works without an internet connection.

Keyboard shortcuts

If you have a keyboard, you can use it to pick numbers. You still tap or click the squares on the board.

1 to 9Select that number. The keys start out using the pen.
0Select C (clear).
. (period)Switch the keys between pen and pencil.
Shift + numberSelect that number with the other tool, just this once. If the keys are using the pen, this uses the pencil, and the other way around.
EscClose this guide.

Tapping a number pad also switches the keys to match that pad.
